About Us

BAUCC

Bangladesh Agricultural University Career Club (BAUCC) is a non-profit, voluntary organization that is independent of politics, religion, race, caste, and creed. It was formed to provide a platform for students of BAU to enhance their professional skills and explore their potential. BAUCC envisions transforming BAU into a hub of change-makers. Its main objective is to bridge the gap between students and resource persons.

The idea of founding BAUCC was initiated by Tanmoy Kumar Ghose, a graduate of Agricultural Economics. Observing the lack of effective career guidance at BAU, Tanmoy, along with his team members - Intisar Awal Prio, Md. Sanowar Hossain, Md. Mehedi Hasan Sourov, Md. Asifur Rahman, and Bappy Majumder approached Dr. Hamidul Islam, Assistant Professor of the Department of Farm Power and Machinery, who agreed to become the club’s president. Tanmoy officially established the club on October 25, 2017, by creating the Facebook group “Bangladesh Agricultural University Career Club (BAUCC).”

Our Teams

Our Departments

Human Resource Manager

The Human Resource Management (HRM) Team deals with member development, well-being, and discipline within the organization. They maintain attendance records, oversee team progress through assigned HRs, and ensure accountability. Along with managing performance evaluations and feedback, they also plan for future organizational growth.

Research and Development

The R&D Team focuses on tracking club progress, identifying strengths and weaknesses, and suggesting improvements. They explore innovative content, build career guidance portfolios, and create strategies to promote sustainable career growth and participation in national or international opportunities.

Communication Lead

The Communication Lead Team deals with internal and external communication of the organization. They handle notices, updates, and collaborations while ensuring information is shared clearly and on time. Their role focuses on making communication transparent, effective, and supportive of the club’s vision.

Digital Content

The Digital Content Creation (DCC) Team is responsible for designing and managing all creative materials, including posters, banners, and online campaigns for the organization. They ensure engaging and appealing content to boost participation in events, maintain consistent branding in collaboration with Media & Relations, and prepare regular reports to track content performance.

Event Management

The Event Management Team plans, organizes, and executes all events and programs. From handling logistics and venue arrangements to coordinating with other teams, they ensure smooth execution and structured programs, serving as the backbone of event success.

Media & Relation

The Media & Relations Team manages the club’s social media presence, uploading content for events and special occasions. They write captions, posts, and press notes, while also networking with other organizations to build partnerships and enhance the organization’s public image.
